Assessment: Assignment 1
Week: 4

Assessed CLO: CLO1

CLO1: Describe the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, including intelligent agents, environments, problem formulation, and state-space representations.

This is an individual assignment.

The purpose of this assignment is to assess whether you can analyze an unfamiliar AI scenario and translate it into the formal concepts studied in the first part of the course.

ImportantAssessment principle

This assignment is not mainly about memorizing definitions.

You are expected to apply AI concepts to a new scenario, justify your choices, and show that your problem formulation is internally consistent.

Scenario — Autonomous Campus Delivery Robot

A university plans to deploy an autonomous robot that delivers small items between campus locations.

The robot may need to travel between places such as:

  • Main Gate;
  • Library;
  • Computer Science Building;
  • Administration;
  • Student Center;
  • Medical Center.

The robot can move through pedestrian paths connecting these locations.

The environment may include:

  • students walking through paths;
  • temporary blocked paths;
  • different travel distances;
  • battery limitations;
  • uncertain travel time;
  • delivery deadlines.

The robot receives a request containing:

  • a pickup location;
  • a destination;
  • an item to deliver.

Its objective is to complete the delivery safely and efficiently.
